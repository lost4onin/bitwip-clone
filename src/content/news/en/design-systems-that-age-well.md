Four years ago we shipped the first version of a design system for a payments client. It has since powered three products, two rebrands and more than 200 screens. Here is what kept it alive.

## Name things after intent, not appearance

`color-surface-raised` survives a rebrand. `grey-200` does not. Every token we named after its role still exists today; most of the ones named after how they looked have been renamed at least once.

## Document the "why", not just the "what"

A component page that explains *when not to use* a pattern saves more time than any prop table.

## Treat accessibility as a feature of the system

Contrast, focus states and motion preferences belong in the tokens themselves, so every team gets them for free.
