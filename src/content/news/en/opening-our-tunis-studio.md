After two years of collaborating remotely with an incredible group of designers and developers in Tunisia, we are making it official: Orbe Studio now has a home in Tunis.

The new studio will focus on product design and front-end engineering, and will work hand in hand with our Paris and Montréal teams.

## We're hiring

We are looking for a senior product designer and two front-end developers. If you love type, performance and accessibility as much as we do, say hello.
