Games are some of the best-designed software in the world: they teach complex systems without a single help page. We spent a month studying them and brought three ideas back into our product work.

## Teach by doing

The first minute of a good game is a tutorial in disguise. Product onboarding can be too.

## Reward small progress

Micro-animations that acknowledge an action make an interface feel responsive and alive — as long as they respect reduced-motion settings.

## Let people fail safely

Undo, drafts and previews make experimentation feel free.
